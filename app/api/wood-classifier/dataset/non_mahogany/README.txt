NON-MAHOGANY IMAGES FOLDER
==========================

Place your non-mahogany wood images here (oak, pine, teak, etc.).

Supported formats:
- .jpg, .jpeg
- .png
- .webp
- .jfif
- .bmp

Tips for best results:
- Use high-quality images (at least 500x500 pixels)
- Good lighting conditions
- Clear, focused shots of wood grain
- Various wood types (oak, pine, walnut, teak, etc.)
- At least 100+ images recommended

You can copy images from:
  machine_learning/images/Non_Mahogany/

Example:
  Copy-Item -Path "../../machine_learning/images/Non_Mahogany/*" -Destination "." -Recurse
